Déjà View loop knob

CPU experiment on the loop count K of NVIDIA's Déjà View / DVLT (Burzio, Fischer et al., arXiv:2605.30215, found via @NVIDIAAI; the post was not fetched). One shared transformer block looped K times is the inference-time compute/quality dial; official code at a pinned commit, weights (non-commercial NVIDIA licence) download at run time. Synthetic multi-view scenes with exact GT cameras and depth, seeds 0/1/2.

  • PARTIAL: latency is linear in K (R² = 0.9998, 0.106 s/loop + 1.10 s fixed) and depth improves 8→16 (AbsRel 0.0615→0.0454), but pose AUC@30 falls 0.693→0.611 — the dial splits by task on this easy domain
  • Pose actually peaks at K=6 (AUC@30 0.700), outside the stated [8,16] validity range; K ∈ {1,2} collapses completely (rotation error ≈ 95–99°)
  • Breaks at low resolution (140²): K=16 is less stable than K=8 — two of four scenes collapse to 52–61° rotation error. More loops amplify failure on degraded inputs

bash setup.sh
.venv/bin/python src/make_scenes.py --out data/scenes
.venv/bin/python src/run_experiment.py && .venv/bin/python src/analyze.py

RaCo rotation keypoints

CPU run of RaCo against ALIKED-N16, both from VisMatch 1.4.0 (Shenoi et al., arXiv:2602.15755, found via @gabriberton; the post was not re-fetched). In-plane rotations of HPatches reference frames, seeds 0/1/2. Weights download at run time and are not vendored.

  • PASS: over 30–150° the repeatability gap is 0.677 (RaCo 0.820, ALIKED 0.143; bar ≥ 0.10). RaCo drops 0.087 from 0° to 135° (bar ≤ 0.10)
  • ALIKED falls from 0.780 at 0° to 0.028 at 90°. At 2 px RaCo stays near 0.80, the shape of the paper's Figure 5, on 4 of the first 20 frames rather than the full AUC
  • Breaks under blur σ = 3 (RaCo 0.21) and on a 64 px canvas, where ALIKED is higher (0.93 vs 0.79). The VisMatch wrapper leaves RaCo's ranker off

e-means vs k-means

CPU run of released faiss.contrib.e_means.NumpyEmeans against faiss.Kmeans (Bessa et al., arXiv:2611.09501, found via @swann_bessa; the post was not re-fetched). Seeds 0/1/2. FAISS is MIT and is not vendored.

  • PARTIAL: at 2.5 epochs the train-MSE ratio is 0.992, 0.999, and 1.000 (bar ≤ 1.01). Wall-clock ratios are 0.47, 0.17, and 0.21 (bar ≤ 0.10)
  • One epoch is still about 3–4% above Lloyd@25. By two epochs every gated mean is inside 1%. On this 4-vCPU box the speedups are 2.1×, 6.1×, and 4.8×
  • Breaks at n=200, d=16 (ratio 1.08, about 31× slower, quality batches of 2). At k=2000, e-means is slightly slower and revives 998 clusters

MuJoCo 4 speculative contacts

CPU experiment on the MuJoCo 4.0.0 speculative-contact flag (discussion #3773, found via @_bbelousov; the post was not re-fetched). A sphere is fired at a 4 mm wall with the flag on and off, seeds 0/1/2, timesteps from 2 ms to 16 ms. MuJoCo is Apache-2.0 and is not vendored.

  • PASS: ON tunneling rate 0.000 at 2–16 ms (bar ≤ 0.05). OFF is 0.005 at 2 ms, 0.443 at 4 ms, 0.792 at 8 ms, 0.958 at 16 ms
  • OFF sits above the kinematic skip curve: a contact whose center has already crossed the far face does not push the sphere back. ON penetration stays about 0.3 mm; OFF energy drift goes from −0.97 to −0.04
  • Constant speed still stops at 1000 m/s and at a 0.5 s step. Both flags tunnel when gravity accelerates a resting sphere through the wall in one step. Per-step cost is 5–20% higher with the flag

Syndrome-mitigation QEC (VEC)

Independent re-implementation of arXiv:2610.12400 (Google Quantum AI, found via @zlatko_minev): reweight shots by MWPM-vs-exact-ML decoder disagreement sectors to cancel residual logical errors in post-processing. Pre-registered d=3/5 experiment at p=0.05, seeds 0/1/2.

  • VEC cuts residual logical bias 32–152× below plain MWPM and 15–101× below postselection on every seed; ⟨Z_L⟩ returns to 1.000±0.001 (ideal +1)
  • PARTIAL: overhead ratio η<4 holds at d=5 (2.4) and d=7 (1.73, near the paper's quoted weights) but not d=3 (5.4–5.7) — matching the paper's distance trend
  • Exact d=5/7 ML via transfer-matrix contraction verified against brute force; breakpoints: empty Y sector at p=0.01, threshold collapse, drift, 0.31%-error syndrome-only noise learning

Correction Training 2D toy

Pedagogical re-implementation of sunovivid/correction-training (arXiv:2610.14136): a stop-gradient corrector iteratively refines the denoiser's prediction at each sampling step. Pre-registered matched-NFE K sweep on 8 Gaussians, seeds 0/1/2.

  • K=0 baseline beats every K∈{1,2,4,8} on MMD² on all three seeds (0.84 vs 3.99 ×10³ at best K, seed 0) — the paper's ImageNet gain inverts on a near-optimal 2D denoiser with no exposure-bias gap to close
  • Breakpoints: ordering holds to K=64 and across NFE 6–240; the one flip is 4 well-separated modes, where K=2 wins. Trains in ~5 s/seed on CPU
  • MIT upstream; no checkpoints downloaded. Prepared (unexecuted) Colab for real SiT-REPA ImageNet sampling — GPU spend needs owner approval
